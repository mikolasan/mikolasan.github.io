There’s a separate door to the basement that is locked in the beginning. Our dad went to travel to the East and left his store to us. In the basement there is a workshop where we can make potions from our garden. The garden is located outside of the shop and originally doesn’t exist or very small or super boring (like only carrots and potatoes there). But we look for recipes, learn ingredients and then search for seeds. Then we plant, water and other farming stuff. 
The workshop already has all the tools but we find a key to unlock it only after some time (strange idea: we pay some fee for the key (maybe our cousin has it or stole it and wants big money instead))
When proper ingredients are put in place then the potion will be ready in 12 hours. But we make like a cauldron in one go, so this will give us 24 bottles (dozen is a nice number).

There will be some global events that will create spikes in demand for some goods and potions too. Potions bring good revenue.

Some ingredients are rare and not possible to grow. But there are travelers and adventurers who bring unique stuff. And people from trade routes and caravans that bring stable supply of rare items. You need to find such suppliers and make contracts with them.

Also once in a while knights and adventurers can visit your shop and sell artifacts and other loot from their quests. When you have developed a few stores already, then the runner will inform you about this guest in one of your stores. You wouldn’t want to miss him. The stuff is usually super cool. This means that it’s pricey and not available anywhere else. So save some money for this occasion and be in time. Some items are collectible and you have a special journal to mark these collections complete. When you sell stuff like this, it increases reputation of your store and more people come and they don’t mind prices higher than usual.

At night you can catch fireflies.

You can sell magical creatures. And probably even grow them on the property beforehand which makes it into tamagotchi like mini game.
