Do you believe in ghosts? Such a common question during Halloween season. My answer is no, I haven't had a single encounter during my long life. No strange silhouettes, no misty forms, no poltergeist activity or even sudden ambient temperature drop. There are few things in my memory that are close to spooky stories, but I never categorized them as such.

I remember once on a birthday party of my good friend we did something like a séance. It was a winter day, when it becomes dark very early. One of our guests was a witch practitioner. She thought us how to levitate a person, and we, about 5 people, raised her in the air just using our forefingers, but stupid birthday boy couldn't clear his thoughts and this experiment failed on him. He wasn't able to concentrate while we were chanting a specific ritual words about a king who died and we need to bury him. He was so fixated on his role of the king, that he started hysterically laughing after every word. Then she did individual sessions in the bathroom in pitch black summoning spirits. So we were standing there holding each other hands in a complete silence trying to catch any sound. Not everyone agreed to this session, maybe because the medium said that she knows how to summon The Queen of Spades, and for that she needs a mirror and it indeed was in the bathroom. If you draw the stairs leading to a door on the mirror and call for her, then she will appear from that door and start choking you. Some guests were very impressed by that possibility. So the bathroom didn't provide the right channeling and we organized a group séance in the living room. Now everyone was there and this time the unexpected thing really happened. We have heard a wolf howl. This event scared the shit out of the birthday boy. He flipped out. Others started panicking too.

But then we heard someone laughing. The howl was not real. The host's parents were in adjacent room, but they easily focused the sound like it was pronounced in the living room in our midst. Despite the wall was concrete it had wall sockets in both rooms that go through the wall, which makes it a trumpet, that was used by the parents to prank us.

That was a memorable birthday party I must say
