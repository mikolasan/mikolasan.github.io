Let's think about anti social app. The one that focused on isolation, anonymity, low-fiction avoidance. It would encourage self-exression more than connection.

So mainly no chat, no replies, no mentions, no followers. You can post but no likes.
Nothing crazy, just _friction-maximizing_ for interaction and _friction-minimizing_ for solitude, which makes it a privacy-first pseudonymous micro-journaling space.

What sociological and philosophical movements considered anti-social?

- Stirnerism or ==Stirnerite== Egoism
- Ayn Rand–style Objectivism
