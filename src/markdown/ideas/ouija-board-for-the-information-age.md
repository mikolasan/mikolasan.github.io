Ouija boards use to be popular in the medium circles as a way of communicating with spirits (_medium_ and _spirits_ have double meaning in English, but I think you know what I mean here) and among teenagers when a company like Hasbro started to mass-produce it.

So this is a PCB board of a credit card size. It has many LEDs for every stop like on Ouija board, which are 26 letters and 10 digits (there also supposed to be Yes and No for short answers, but the digital version skipped them).
